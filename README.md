# WOW

A free Mac menu bar reminder app with a little 3D pet that walks on your screen and brings your reminders to you.

**Website:** https://lalitpatel11.github.io/WOW-site/
**Download:** [WOW.dmg](https://github.com/lalitpatel11/WOW-site/releases/latest/download/WOW.dmg) · macOS 13 or later

## Install

1. Open `WOW.dmg` and drag **WOW** into **Applications**.
2. Open WOW. macOS warns that it can't verify the app, because WOW isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer account. Click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.

Or run this in Terminal:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/WOW.app
```
