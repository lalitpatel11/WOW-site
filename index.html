Install

Up and running in a minute.

WOW isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer account yet, so macOS asks you to allow it once.

01

Download

Get WOW.dmg, open it, and drag WOW into your Applications folder.

02

Allow it once

Open WOW. When macOS warns you, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

03

Say hi

Look for the 🔔 in your menu bar. The welcome tour helps you pick a pet and your first reminder.

Prefer Terminal? This removes the download warning:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/WOW.app

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is WOW really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no paid tier.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac on macOS 13 Ventura or later, with Apple silicon or Intel.

Why does macOS say it can't verify WOW?

Apple charges developers a yearly fee to "notarize" apps. WOW isn't notarized yet, so macOS shows a warning the first time. Allow it once in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, and it opens normally after that.

How do I get new versions?

WOW checks for updates once a day and asks before installing anything. You can also check yourself in General → Check for Updates. On version 1.0 or 1.1? Download WOW once more from this page; after that, updates come to you.

Does it collect my data?

No. WOW has no analytics and no account. The only time it goes online is the daily update check, which sends nothing about you. Your reminders and stats stay on your Mac.

Will the pet slow my Mac down?

No. The pet uses lightweight pre-rendered frames instead of a live 3D engine. You can pause it or turn it off anytime from the menu bar.

Something's broken. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub and we'll take a look.
